# B — Native

1. Reps already know iOS, so the portal borrows its grammar: a large-title header, inset grouped lists, Settings-style icon tiles, a Fitness ring for the challenge, a floating tab bar, and Log Sale as a page sheet (a centered form sheet over the dimmed dashboard on desktop).
2. One surface language: navy-tinted near-black steps (`--bg` → `--surface` → `--fill`), taken from the brand's #061735 ink, with lime as the only tint colour. Amber means "check this" and blue means "scheduled". Nothing else carries colour.
3. Geist, not Archivo: its metrics are close to SF, so it reads as native, and its tabular figures keep money columns aligned. The ramp is iOS's (34/22/20/17/15/13/12), with 12px as the floor.
4. Exactly one primary button (lime, navy text, 52px, the marketing site's pairing), one secondary (fill, 52px) and one chip (a 44px segmented control). All are defined as tokens at the top of `native.module.css`.
5. Screenshot-first Log Sale: the scan shows its reads on the thumbnail the way Live Text does. In review, each flagged field has an amber rule, a "Check this" pill and a plain-language reason, and the matching lines on the proof screenshot are marked in amber too.
