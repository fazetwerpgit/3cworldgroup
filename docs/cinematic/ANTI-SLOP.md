# Anti-slop checklist — the bar every round is judged against

Owner's words (2026-09-21): "if I come back and this looks like AI slop" the project is over.
Research summary (developersdigest, 925studios, smoothui, vibecodekit, dev.to "purple gradient
problem", 2026): AI-built sites converge on the statistical average of templates. The tells
below are the ones designers name. Where our approved direction *touches* a tell, the
mitigation is written next to it — direction is not up for debate, execution is.

## Hard tells — none may appear anywhere on the site
- Purple/blue gradients, aurora, glow orbs, floating particles, starfield, mesh blobs, Tron grid,
  neon, glassmorphism/frosted cards, colored box-shadow glows, HUD decoration.
- Inter/system fonts; serif-italic accent word; badge/pill sitting directly above the H1.
- Centered hero + single floating CTA; hero → 3 cards → logo strip → pricing → FAQ → footer.
- Rows of identical icon-on-top cards with colored top/left borders and uniform 16px radius.
- Stat banner rows of big numbers ("50+", "98%") — especially unverified ones.
- Vague aspirational headline copy ("Build the future"); fake logos, testimonials, metrics.
- Stock "diverse team at laptops"; plastic symmetrical AI illustration; generated people.
- Same fade-up reveal on every block at identical timing; missing hover/active/focus states.
- Dark mode with mid-grey body text under AA contrast; microtype under 12px; widows/orphans.
- Empty vertical voids, stair-step or mismatched section seams, photos crushed to mud.

## Where our direction touches a tell — and what makes it authored instead
- **Navy ground + Bebas uppercase.** Risk: "permanent dark mode + all-caps labels." Mitigation:
  navy alternates with paper on every page on straight, full-bleed edges (owner, 2026-09-21:
  the diagonal seam is retired — "the repeated diagonal section cuts"); the joint is carried
  by the background change, the standard section pad on both sides, and the hairline lead-in
  under every section head; uppercase is reserved for
  display and eyebrows, body is sentence-case Geist at ≥16px with measured contrast; lime is
  used as a single accent line, never a glow.
- **Eyebrow over the headline.** Risk: "badge above H1." Mitigation: it is a tracked text
  label on the baseline grid, never a pill, never bordered, never colored-background.
- **01/02/03 numerals.** Risk: "numbered 1-2-3 steps." Mitigation: the numeral is the
  typographic weight of the row (large Bebas, lime-ink), used in at most one section per
  page, and never combined with icon cards.
- **Scroll reveals.** Mitigation: two authored entrances (heads wipe, bodies rise), staggered,
  fire once, plus one real set piece per page (the route line draw, the chapter stage, the
  3C mark).
- **Photography.** Mitigation: environments only, dusk grade, one photo per placement, local
  scrims behind copy only; every hero photo is a different picture.

## What "authored" looks like on this site (positive tests)
- Every page has one moment you could not get from a template: Home's route draw,
  Contact's 3C mark line landing on the hairline, Services' split rows with lit photos,
  Careers' front-door hero.
- Rhythm: no two adjacent sections share the same grid; section heads share ONE grammar.
- Type: display lines break on meaning, no single-word last lines, lime line shorter than or
  equal to the white line above it.
- Details: hover/active/focus states on every interactive element; hairlines tuned per
  ground; select arrow, form states, mobile apply bar all designed, not defaulted.
- Copy sounds like the company's owner, not a brochure: specific, plain, no hype.
